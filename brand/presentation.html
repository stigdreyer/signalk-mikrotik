What we heard

The brief

A Signal K plugin that switches a boat's MikroTik router to an allowlist while the internet is metered (Starlink pay-per-GB), and shows where the data goes. Used by skippers and crew on phones and chart-side tablets, day and night watch.

dependablecalmseaworthywatchful

Success: reads at 16 px, works in one colour and red on black at night, avoids security clichés (shields, padlocks, walls, flames), and means something to people on boats.

Concept A

Valve

A boat's through-hull valve (seacock), turned part-way open: the crew decides how much flows.

  • A control every sailor knows: open or shut by choice, which is what the plugin does to the router
  • Turned 45 degrees, part-way open, it says metered without drawing a speedometer
  • Ring, lever and pivot on one 256 grid, one stroke weight; a heavier cut for 16 to 24 px
  • The lever carries the colour: the part the crew controls
